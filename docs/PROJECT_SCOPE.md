# Project Scope

## Problem

Users may know that a legal problem exists without knowing its broad legal category, likely next step, or relevant professional specialization.

## Objective

Build an AI-assisted legal triage interface that converts a plain-language description into understandable, structured legal information and demonstrates routing toward relevant legal domains.

## In scope

- legal query intake;
- Gemini-assisted analysis;
- domain and priority explanation;
- recommended next steps;
- document-analysis workflow;
- sample lawyer specialization matching.

## Out of scope

- binding legal advice;
- guaranteed outcomes;
- autonomous court filing;
- official Aadhaar authentication;
- a verified national lawyer registry;
- unsupported population-level ML accuracy claims.

## Current research status

The active implementation is a Gemini-based generative-AI prototype. A standalone trained ML model is not stored in the repository.
